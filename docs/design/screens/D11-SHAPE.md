# D11 Clip Replay — approved Pen.dev handoff

On 25 November 2026, the user approved **A · Context above replay with standard controls**. Use the browser video element, native range scrubber, and RAFIQ destructive confirmation treatment (**D11-C01, D11-C04, D11-C06**). D11 is an **Operate** workspace. A signed-in driver reviewing a completed session while stationary can identify the incident, play and seek through its private video-only clip, return to the source, read its expiry, and delete the clip. Put **Back to clips** or **Back to incident** above the **Clip replay** heading. Use **Back to clips** for an authorized direct entry with no incident origin. Place **View source session** in the evidence strip as its own D07 destination.
2. **Evidence strip.** In reading order, show the behavior labels and incident date/time, clip capture date/time and playable duration, source-session reference, and **Expires** with date/time and zone. Use the time zone applied to the review. Keep incident time, clip capture time and playback position separately labeled. Include every recorded behavior for a combined incident. Put **Available first segment** next to the recorded duration for a truncated clip. Use the reported capture timestamp and duration until the source contract confirms the wall-clock recording start and end.
3. **Player.** Place a dark video viewport directly below the evidence strip. Preserve the recorded aspect ratio with letterboxing as needed. Use `spacing.sm` (8 px) within evidence and actions, `spacing.md` (16 px) for group padding and control spacing, `spacing.xl` (24 px) between sections, and `rounded.md` (8 px) where existing panel and feedback patterns use it. ## Playback and interaction

- Open at `0:00` in a ready, stopped state. A deliberate **Play** action initiates authenticated media loading. Keep playback user-started and controls visible. Once actually playing, offer **Pause**. After the clip ends, keep the last eligible frame and final position and show **Replay**. Replay starts at zero on request. Keep the player focused on the silent recording and essential transport controls.
- Enable seeking when media duration and a seekable range are known. Drag, click/tap or use one-second arrow steps on the focused scrubber. Home and End go to the seekable endpoints. Provide the ±5-second buttons as discrete alternatives to dragging and clamp their results. Show the requested position while dragging and commit on release. Preserve pause when the driver seeks while paused. A playing seek can resume the prior play intent after the new position is ready and authorization still holds. Name buttons and expose a value such as “4 seconds of 10 seconds.” Avoid page-wide shortcuts and per-tick announcements. [Media seek slider pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/slider/examples/slider-seek/)
- Back revalidates current authority and restores D10’s filters, page, scroll and row, or D07’s timeline range, D08 occurrence, scroll and opener. When the clip action has disappeared, focus the authorized incident’s clip status or heading, or D10’s nearest surviving row or results heading. The source-session link navigates independently to the authorized D07 record. [Shared design rules](../../SCREENS.md#shared-design-rules)

## States and copy

| State | Pen presentation and intended browser behavior |
|---|---|
| Checking access | Show shell, **Loading clip** and static evidence/player placeholders. Reveal private context only after revalidation. |
| Ready, playing, paused, seeking and ended | Keep the controls in the same position. Show the appropriate transport action, numeric position, thumb, and focus indicator. Stylize the displayed footage only through the existing viewport boundary. Use synthetic evidence in Pen. |
| Initial load and buffering | Use **Loading video…** and **Cancel** when the first fetch can be aborted. Show **Buffering…** for an authorized playback stall, retaining the last eligible frame and the fixed control region. |
| Pending, retryable or unavailable | Show **Clip pending** when an authorized state indicates pending media. For a transient playback failure, keep eligible context and offer **Retry playback** after revalidation. For a terminal playback failure, show **Playback unavailable** with a safe known reason when available. Remove the frame and seek controls when playback is unavailable. Offer the authorized source or return link. |
| Access revoked, expired or inaccessible protected link | Stop playback immediately on known eligibility loss. Abort media reads, clear the video source, decoded frame and in-memory object URL, and close any clip dialog. Use the P08 heading **This item is unavailable.** with the appropriate authorized return link. On account change, sign-out or account-level restriction, clear all private clip and restoration state and follow the existing account flow. Place focus at the safe heading. Show a specific item-level cause only within separately authorized context, such as D08. |
| Delete confirmation | Pause on open. Use **Delete this clip?** and identify the clip with behavior and exact incident time. Suggested body: “This removes replay and access through clip links. Your session and incident record remain. RAFIQ withdraws any hosted report that depends on this clip.” Use **Cancel** and **Delete clip**. Focus Cancel, contain focus, allow Escape before submission, and restore focus to the opener on dismissal. Playback stays paused. |
| Deleting, failure and success | Show **Deleting…**, prevent duplicate submissions, and announce progress once. On a recoverable failure, show scoped feedback and retain focus within the dialog. For an uncertain response, verify the state before offering retry. On confirmed deletion, clear private media and return to the authorized incident or filtered clips. Announce **Clip deleted** and focus the nearest surviving context. |

Show hosted expiry at point of use. Hosted clips expire seven days after capture. Verified queued clips expire 24 hours after capture. Independent session and incident metadata expires 90 days after session end. Retained copies are purged within 30 days of expiry or deletion. ## Motion, accessibility and Pen variants

- **On Play.** At activation, show loading immediately if media is still being fetched. Once playback starts, the footage and the position change at their actual media timing. The scrubber tracks the requested pointer or key position without interpolation. On pause or end, update controls in place. On expiry or access loss, clear the frame and controls immediately. Dialog, loading, retry and deletion states appear in place without animation. Reduced motion uses the same visual states and keeps video playback explicit.
- **Accessibility.** Preserve landmarks, skip link, heading order, keyboard access and visible focus. Use text and a non-color cue for state changes, readable time values and appropriately sized pointer targets. Announce load results, terminal transitions and delete outcomes once. Link to the recorded incident description in D08 where available. Verify whether it conveys the significant visual evidence in the silent clip and derive any further equivalent description from an authorized source with the same access and retention controls. [Experience accessibility floor](../../EXPERIENCE.md#accessibility-floor) · [WAI prerecorded video-only guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/audio-only-and-video-only-prerecorded.html)
- **Pen frames.** Show 1440 px ready and paused mid-clip, a focused seek state, a combined incident with a truncated clip, 390 px and 320 px stacked views, loading and eligible retry, an authorized playback unavailable state, a P08 replacement after in-view revocation, and delete confirmation, progress, failure and return. Use editable layers and a neutral placeholder or synthetic still. Include elapsed and total labels in the example. The browser owns authenticated media, keyboard interaction, focus, announcements, network behavior and authority enforcement.

## Acceptance checks and open contracts

- Both D10 and D08 open the same eligible clip with its incident, capture timing, source and expiry legible before playback. First load and Back leave playback stopped. Keyboard and pointer can operate every control. At 200% zoom and 320 CSS px, context, playback controls and deletion reflow in order.
- Induce an eligible fetch failure, an unplayable clip and an in-view revocation. Verify retry, scoped unavailability and P08 respectively. Verify frame and source cleanup on known loss, including during loading or seeking, and reject a delayed response after the account switches. Confirm the server-to-browser revocation signal or revalidation cadence for media already loaded in memory, including offline behavior. Keep server reads authenticated and qualify WebM/VP8 playback and seek on the reference laptop.
- Authorize the `getClip`, `getIncident` and session reads together for D11 evidence. Confirm the definition of `capturedAt`. A scrubber incident marker needs a reliable event-to-clip offset. Use capture time and duration as the verified timing fields.
- Exercise cancel, confirmed delete, recoverable failure and a lost deletion response. Verify expiry and deletion while the clip is open, valid Back restoration and preservation of independently retained incident and session evidence.
