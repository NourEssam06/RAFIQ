# D11 Clip Replay — design proposal and component research

**Research and alternatives · 25 November 2026**

D11 is a stationary, owner-only review workspace for a clip from a completed session. The driver should be able to identify the incident, play and seek through the recorded segment, return to its source, and understand when replay access ends. This is an Impeccable **Operate** surface. The proposal supplies a Pen.dev direction for approval. ## Evidence and playback

- **Incident.** Use the recorded behavior labels, including all labels for a combined occurrence, and the authorized incident date and time with zone. Keep capture timestamp, recorded duration, and player position distinct. Show **Available first segment** next to the duration for `truncated`. Use the recorded duration for the context and the playable media duration for the seek range once known. Show exact expiry with date, time and zone. Hosted clips expire seven days from capture. Pending queue expiry is 24 hours from capture when corroborated, and independent session/incident metadata has its own 90-day retention. Use a readable **View source session** link to D07 when eligible. [Screen requirements](../../SCREENS.md#d11-clip-replay) · [Clip lifecycle](../../PRD.md#86-clip-and-report-lifecycle)
- **Player.** Use a plain video viewport, original aspect ratio and letterboxing as needed, with a visible **Play** button, an elapsed/total readout such as `0:04 / 0:10`, and one scrubber outside the footage. Start at `0:00` and fetch private media on the first Play request. Keep controls present through playback and pause. Label the player **Video only**. Volume, looping, hover thumbnails, animated overlays, picture-in-picture, and sharing are outside the proposed control set. Keep the last frame and final position while access is current and change the primary action to **Replay** at the end. Replay starts at zero on request. [HTML video element](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Reference/Elements/video)
- **Seek.** Enable seeking once duration and a seekable range are known. Click/tap to seek, drag the thumb, or use the focused slider’s arrow keys (proposed one-second steps) and Home/End. Include labeled **Back 5 seconds** and **Forward 5 seconds** as discrete alternatives to dragging, clamped to the playable interval. While dragging, show the requested time immediately and commit on release. Keep paused playback paused; during a playing scrub, resume only according to the prior authorized playback intent and current eligibility. Use a visible focus ring and announce the seek value in time words, such as “4 seconds of 10 seconds.” Avoid announcing every playback tick. [WAI media seek slider](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/slider/examples/slider-seek/)
- **Back and source.** **Back to clips** restores D10’s current-authority filters, page, scroll and row. **Back to incident** restores D07’s range, D08 occurrence, scroll and the opener where eligible. The source-session link is a distinct destination. After deletion or expiry, focus the surviving D08 clip status or incident heading, or the nearest surviving D10 row or results heading. Revalidate the return destination and restoration state. [Shared navigation rules](../../SCREENS.md#shared-design-rules) · [D08 handoff](D08-SHAPE.md) · [D10 handoff](D10-SHAPE.md)

## States and deletion

| State | Presentation and behavior |
|---|---|
| Checking access | Show the shell and a static placeholder with **Loading clip**. Reveal evidence after current authorization. |
| Ready, playing, paused, seeking, ended | Keep the control bar in place. Show actual play/pause state and readable position. During a short seek, show the requested position and then settle to the confirmed media time. |
| Loading playback | Retain authorized context and viewport size. Show **Loading video…** beside the controls and **Cancel** while the initial fetch can be aborted. For playback buffering, retain the last authorized frame and label **Buffering…**. Announce each meaningful transition once. |
| Pending or recoverable playback failure | Use **Clip pending** for verified pending state. Distinguish authorized offline or transfer failure from an empty clip. Use **Retry playback** only for a retryable failure with current eligibility. Revalidate before reopening media. Return to paused readiness after recovery if the previous play request is no longer current. |
| Playback unavailable with authorized context | Replace the player with **Playback unavailable**. Use a verified item-level reason where safe, including expiry when still disclosed in an eligible incident. Keep separately authorized context and navigation. Show a retry action only where the cause is recoverable. |
| Access revoked or protected clip no longer eligible | Immediately stop playback, abort fetches, detach and clear the source and last frame, revoke the in-memory media URL, and discard clip state. Close any clip dialog. Use P08’s **This item is unavailable.** and its authorized return link. Strip private context on sign-out, account switch or broader eligibility loss, then follow the existing account flow. Focus the safe destination’s heading and announce the transition once. |
| Confirm, deleting, failure, success | Opening **Delete clip** pauses playback. Use **Delete this clip?** with the behavior and exact incident date/time. Explain that replay and linked access end, the independent session and incident remain, and any dependent hosted report is withdrawn. Focus **Cancel**, trap focus, allow Escape before submission, and return focus to the opener on cancellation. Leave playback paused on cancel. On submit show **Deleting…** and guard duplicate activation. On a recoverable error show inline feedback and a retry when eligible. After an uncertain result, revalidate before offering retry. On confirmed deletion, clear media, return to surviving D08 or D10 context and announce **Clip deleted** there. |

Use the same generic P08 treatment for an unauthorized direct URL, a removed clip, or an expired protected clip whose metadata is no longer visible. Keep past-frame dismissal immediate, including in reduced motion. ## Pen frames and implementation checks

- **Reusable RAFIQ components.** Private shell, headings, focus and link treatments, button styles, status labels, dividers, static loading treatment, P08, and destructive dialog anatomy. **Standard controls.** Buttons, range, time text, modal and inline status. **New RAFIQ composition.** Incident and retention strip, evidence player with its fixed control row, and clip-specific confirmation copy/states. External candidates are optional references.
- **Pen variants.** Show authorized ready desktop with synthetic evidence, a paused seek example, combined behaviors and truncated duration, 390 px and 320 CSS px reflow, loading or buffering, eligible retry, unavailable playback with authorized context, the P08 replacement, and delete confirmation, pending, failure and confirmed return. Keep visuals editable. Use a neutral placeholder or synthetic still in the footage slot. The playback itself, keyboard semantics, networking, permissions and runtime cleanup belong to the browser implementation.
- **Accessibility.** Give the video region and controls names, use a skip link and logical focus order, keep metadata and controls readable under zoom, and use text plus a non-color cue for critical states. D08 supplies the accessible incident description. Verify whether it conveys the meaningful visual evidence of the prerecorded clip. Any additional equivalent description needs an authorized source and the same retention and access controls. [RAFIQ accessibility floor](../../EXPERIENCE.md#accessibility-floor) · [WAI prerecorded video-only guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/audio-only-and-video-only-prerecorded.html)
- **Data and playback gates.** `getClip` supplies clip and parent IDs, `capturedAt`, `expiresAt`, `durationMs`, `truncated` and safe state. Join current-authority `getIncident` and session context for behavior and source timing. Confirm whether `capturedAt` denotes the first frame before labeling a wall-clock recording interval. Use the recorded capture timestamp and duration until that meaning is confirmed. Omit an incident tick on the scrubber until an authoritative event-to-clip offset exists. Confirm how the active browser learns of remote deletion or revocation after fetching media, including when offline. The API specifies denial and local cleanup on known loss. Qualify video-only WebM/VP8, duration and seeking on the supported laptop. Test expiry during playback and seek, delayed responses after account switch, and deletion races.
