# D04 Emergency Mode

**Approved design direction · Operate · Embedded in D03 Monitoring**

During an eligible supervised session, the stationary driver or authorized supervisor needs to know whether a contact alert is about to be attempted, how to stop it, and what evidence RAFIQ has for each channel. D04 starts inside Monitoring after readiness or a manual resume. Sustained qualifying drowsiness alone can start the countdown. If setup finds no approved supervision or no eligible channel, D02 explains the reason and D03 still shows ordinary warnings. If eligibility changes during a sequence, keep the outcome visible in D03 as permitted by the current presentation.

## Approved layout and presentation

**Approved on 25 November 2026.** Use layout A, stacked at all widths, and **D04-C00** for immediate text and icon updates. The approval includes prioritizing the direct cancellation action ahead of warning acknowledgment.

The active panel occupies D03’s full-width warning area as a separately labeled region. After session and observation status, place D04 ahead of the ordinary warning and its **Acknowledge warning** action. Stack **Emergency Mode**, **Contact alert countdown**, **12 seconds left to cancel**, and **Cancel contact alert** in one left-aligned column. Place the button directly below the time in every frame, including the passive presentation. The stacked layout keeps the action close to the time and in a predictable location as the viewport changes. The side-by-side desktop option was reviewed as a way to save vertical space.

Put **Standing by** and read-only attempt outcomes in D03’s supporting status area beside Recording and Sync. Preserve the current result in the urgent slot while it holds focus. The stationary preview and supporting content follow the time-critical controls on reflow.

Use the current D03 canvas and cream ink, its panel boundary and existing filled/outline control treatments, Mori or the approved Space Grotesk fallback, `{spacing.md}` panel padding, `{spacing.base}` within the panel, and `{rounded.md}` panel corners. Give **Cancel contact alert** the strongest button emphasis while it is available. Keep **Acknowledge warning** in the warning region with secondary action emphasis and retain D03’s visible warning severity. Use tabular figures and reserve enough width for the full timer phrase. Use steady icons and text for states and reserve the delivery confirmation checkmark for **Delivered** within the evidence rows. ## Sequence and channel states

Sequence headings explain whether the countdown or further attempts can proceed. Channel rows report evidence for a particular contact and channel. A closed sequence can contain different channel outcomes. Group up to four recorded attempts under **Contact 1** and **Contact 2** in configured order. Use order labels without names, addresses, phone numbers, or location in live fixtures. Mark a known withheld channel only when evidence identifies it. For example, **Contact 1 · Email · Accepted** and **WhatsApp · Failed** can coexist. Compact text rows fit the live panel and reflow to separate lines. D08, opened from D07’s emergency marker or equivalent event list after the session, carries the chronological record, elapsed session timestamps, and later evidence. Use an ordered list of recorded events in D08 with event label, contact order, channel and timestamp where applicable.

| State | Proposed visible copy | Meaning and handling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Standing by** | **Standing by** · “Contact alerts are available for sustained drowsiness.” | Quiet state for an eligible active session. Show no timer or cancellation control. Ordinary warnings stay independent. |
| **Countdown** | **Contact alert countdown** · **12 seconds left to cancel** · **Cancel contact alert** | Display the time from the active cancellation deadline. One activation by an authorized person stops unsent attempts. **Acknowledge warning** leaves the countdown intact. |
| **Canceled** | **Contact alert canceled** · “No contact alert was sent” when established. | Give immediate local feedback when cancellation takes effect. If dispatch may already have been claimed, use the evidence boundary below. Keep the warning and its acknowledgment state independent. |
| **Withheld** | **Contact alert withheld** · “RAFIQ went offline before this attempt.” | A send was prevented before dispatch. Show the verified reason when available, such as offline, stale contact eligibility, loss of supervisor authorization or active-tab confirmation, or expiry before an attempt. Use neutral wording when the reason is unavailable. |
| **Closed** | **Contact alert sequence closed** · “Monitoring paused. Further attempts stopped.” | Use the known reason, such as observed recovery, monitoring pause, session end, delivery, or expiry. Keep any recorded channel rows visible. Use **Further attempts stopped** for an already active sequence whose prior outcomes are still relevant. |
| **Pending** | **Pending** · “Attempt in progress.” | A recorded attempt is in progress. While sequence status is being checked and no attempt is established, use **Checking contact alert status** at the sequence level. |
| **Accepted** | **Accepted** · “Provider accepted the alert. Awaiting delivery confirmation.” | Provider processing is known. Use a neutral inbox or queue cue. |
| **Delivered** | **Delivered** · “Provider confirmed delivery.” | Positive delivery evidence for this channel. Shows neither reading nor response as an inferred outcome. |
| **Failed** | **Failed** · “This channel reported a final failure.” | Terminal failure is established. It counts toward fallback eligibility for this contact. |
| **Unconfirmed** | **Unconfirmed** · “Delivery could not be confirmed.” | An attempt exists and definitive evidence is still missing when the deadline closes it. Keep the state neutral and distinct from Withheld and Failed. |

The controlled-test starting values are **10 seconds** of continuous qualifying drowsiness, then **15 seconds** to cancel, with a **120-second** attempt deadline measured from countdown entry. Show only the cancellation window as the live countdown. Read the current deadline on render and after browser scheduling or visibility changes. At expiry, switch immediately to the applicable sequence status without displaying a lingering zero or using the animation as permission to send. Camera pause clears dwell and countdown. Manual resume with usable observation starts fresh qualification and a full window.

**Cancel contact alert** is a single native button with no confirmation or long press. Activation invalidates local authority immediately. A pre-dispatch cancellation can show the canceled result immediately. If cancellation and dispatch overlap and an attempt may have been claimed, say **Further attempts stopped** and retain the known channel states. If the result is ambiguous, use a bounded status read and keep the uncertainty visible. Show **No contact alert was sent** only when the evidence establishes that no attempt reached dispatch. Never offer a resend from the panel or let a status read or reconnect restart a stale sequence.

For every attempt, including fallback, the active tab must provide fresh authenticated online confirmation. Skip contacts with zero eligible channels. For the current contact, each eligible channel can be attempted at most once. Confirmed delivery on either channel stops progression. Advance to the next contact only when all eligible channels for the current contact have been attempted and every one has terminally failed. Pending, Accepted and other ambiguous outcomes hold progression. At expiry, only unresolved attempted channels become **Unconfirmed**. Late authorized evidence can update the recorded outcome without restarting progression. Clicking **End session** leads to D05 or ready D07 and stops unsent attempts. D21 owns contact configuration and X01 owns recipient delivery.

## Accessibility and motion

- Keep the button and readable seconds visible on the first countdown render. Use the countdown as labeled, readable `timer` content outside the live announcement region. Announce countdown entry once with the action name. Reserve assertive announcements for the urgent transition and coordinate them with D03 warning escalation. Politely announce cancellation, withholding, and meaningful channel outcomes once as applicable. Keep timer ticks silent and the current value available on demand.
- Follow the visual order in the DOM. Keep cancellation the first applicable action in the warning area while preserving existing focus on countdown entry and during tick or evidence updates. `Enter` and `Space` activate the focused button. When cancellation is focused and ceases to be available, keep that context by focusing its successor status in the same panel, then allow normal keyboard continuation. When access is revoked, move focus to a surviving authorized target or labeled status. If D03’s stationary leave confirmation is open at countdown entry, the proposed behavior is to dismiss it, retain the session and move focus from the removed dialog to the **Contact alert countdown** heading, with cancellation next in tab order.
- Use steady text, shape and contrast for urgency. Keep target size, focus visibility and wrapped text intact at 900 px, 200% zoom and 320 CSS px reflow. At narrow widths, show the timer and cancellation before the warning’s long cue list and before the preview. Test with keyboard from above and below the region, long warnings, and a viewport already scrolled away from the warning area. Verify the active control is reachable in time under the approved procedure.
- During supervised motion, use the institution-approved passive D03 presentation. The driver remains passive. Only the authorized supervisor on that device can use cancellation, warning acknowledgment, or pause when applicable. Suppress the stationary preview, navigation, and optional motion. Use the same immediate state changes with reduced motion, sound off, and forced colors. D04 needs no entrance, blink, rolling digits, spinner, or delivery animation.

## Research and component selection

- **Countdown and emergency status.** Apple’s Emergency SOS separates a countdown from the resultant action, and it documents cancellation of emergency-contact notification. For RAFIQ, pair readable seconds with immediate cancellation on the same stationary or delegated panel. GOV.UK’s warning text uses a recognizable symbol plus explicit wording. Use that correspondence for urgency while keeping the warning and contact alert visually identifiable as separate actions. [Apple Emergency SOS](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/guide/personal-safety/ips4f0cd709b/1.0/web/1.0) · [GOV.UK warning text](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/warning-text/)
- **Contact delivery timeline.** Resend logs events and distinguishes API success, delivery to the recipient’s mail server, failure and opening. USWDS recommends a step indicator for linear flows. RAFIQ’s channels can overlap, fall back conditionally, and receive late evidence, so use independent status rows in D04 and a chronological event list in D08. [Resend event types](https://resend.com/docs/webhooks/event-types) · [Resend webhook event log](https://resend.com/changelog/webhook-event-visibility) · [USWDS step indicator guidance](https://designsystem.digital.gov/components/step-indicator/)

## Pen.dev fixtures and acceptance

Create editable D04 variants inside copies of the D03 contexts for 1440 px stationary desktop, 900 px laptop, 320 CSS px reflow, and passive supervised simulator. Include authorized and revoked passive fixtures. Illustrate the entire status table with text and icon layers and use synthetic data. Show storyboard frames for **15 seconds → 12 seconds → 1 second → result** and **countdown → Cancel → Canceled**. Each frame is discrete and the button already appears in the first applicable frame. Pen depicts hierarchy and states. The browser and API supply deadlines, accessible announcements and focus handling, authority, cancellation, and delivery evidence.

- **Trigger and action.** Verify sustained qualifying drowsiness opens a full window. Check ordinary warning acknowledgment during the countdown, direct cancellation by keyboard, offline cancellation, a long combined warning at reflow, and an open leave confirmation. Verify non-drowsiness triggers alone leave emergency dispatch inactive.
- **Invalidation and race.** Cover recovery, camera loss → manual resume, pause, session end, delegation revocation, contact edit, and reconnect. Exercise cancel immediately before expiry and when dispatch may have been claimed. Confirm the visible outcome only asserts what the evidence establishes. Verify no stale sends or duplicate provider calls.
- **Delivery and history.** Cover email only, WhatsApp only, both channels, no eligible channel, mixed Accepted and Failed, Delivered plus Failed, all terminal failures leading to an eligible second contact, and an ambiguous attempt expiring to Unconfirmed. Check Withheld distinct from a recorded attempt, late evidence without renewed dispatch, and the path from D07’s event list to D08.
- **Accessibility and passive presentation.** Check Chrome on Windows with keyboard and a screen reader. Read the timer on demand and hear each meaningful transition once. Verify no stale focus, no obscured cancellation, and legibility with reduced motion and forced colors. Run controlled stationary and simulator fixtures before qualifying any supervised motion use or live channel.
