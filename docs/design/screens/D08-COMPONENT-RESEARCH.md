# D08 Incident and alert details

**Shaping proposal · Operate mode · 25 November 2026**

When a signed-in driver reviews a completed session, D08 answers what RAFIQ observed at a particular moment, what warning or contact alert followed, and what evidence is available. The driver should be able to distinguish a detection from an issued warning and a provider’s acceptance from confirmed delivery. D08 opens within D07 from a timeline marker or its equivalent entry in the ordered event list. [Screen requirements](../../SCREENS.md#d08-incident-and-alert-details) · [Product requirements](../../PRD.md#54-history-recordings-and-data-lifecycle) · [Experience](../../EXPERIENCE.md#review-analysis-and-data-lifecycle)

## Layout and disclosure

**Selected layout.** Keep the timeline at full width and place D08 immediately below it, before the ordered event list. The panel is a named section in D07’s reading order. Its opening preserves the timeline’s layout, and switching occurrences reuses the same panel. Desktop has room to place timing facts and clip availability side by side. At narrower widths the facts and actions stack, while the selected occurrence remains identifiable by its event label and exact elapsed time. This follows the D07 inline proposal and lets drivers compare overlaps in the full-width lanes. The extra vertical travel from the event list is handled by an explicit **Read selected details** jump. [D07 shaping proposal](D07-COMPONENT-RESEARCH.md)

**Alternative reviewed.** A wide-screen side rail could keep the event list and selected details alongside the plot for repeated review. It would reduce the plot width when markers are dense and increase wrapping in the delivery history. The rail would stack below the plot at narrow widths and browser zoom. An overlay sheet served as a motion reference during research. The two layouts considered for D08 both keep review in the page flow.

```text
D07  Timeline · Elapsed time since session start · Range controls
     Observation and behavior lanes · Selected marker + exact time
     Read selected details

D08  [Occurrence type and recorded label] · [Exact elapsed time]
     Local time + zone when useful       Back to selected time · Close details
     Key evidence for the selected occurrence
     Supporting measurements / Delivery updates, where available
     Clip state, expiry and Open clip, where eligible

D07  Chronological events · Selected row + exact time
```

**Selected disclosure.** Keep core timing, behaviors, overlap, confidence, warning severity, latest channel outcomes and clip state visible when applicable. Use independent, keyboard-operable disclosures for supporting measurements and recorded delivery updates. Show one concise availability line when a section has no authorized data. Collapsed sections can say what they contain, for example **Supporting measurements (3)** when the count is known. Use one neutral panel with dividers and plain labels for its sections.

**Visual authority.** Read-only inspection of [RAFIQ.pen](<../../../Pen Design/RAFIQ.pen>) found the D01 desktop `v2yld`, narrow `bhsYb`, timeline focus board `NjEkM` and D06 desktop `m14mRu` frames. The file had no named D07 or D08 frames. Use D01’s plot, selected marker and ordered event treatment and D06’s Sessions navigation. Match the existing Space Grotesk substitute and the documented Mori hierarchy. Use `colors.canvas` (#0e100f), `colors.ink` (#fffce1) and `colors.accent-stone` (#bbbaa6), `spacing.sm` (8 px) between related values, `spacing.md` (16 px) inside groups, `spacing.xl` (24 px) between groups, and `rounded.md` (8 px) for the panel. Inherit established focus and status treatments. Pair each severity or outcome with text and its applicable icon or shape. [Design foundations](../../DESIGN.md) · [Approved D06 shape](D06-SHAPE.md)

## What the panel shows

| Selected occurrence | Visible first | Details and evidence rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Incident | Exact selected elapsed time, local time and zone where useful, incident start, end and duration, constituent behaviors and their elapsed spans, detector confidence per detection, and associated warnings with recorded severity. | Identify concurrent behaviors and their actual overlap window and duration. Use the original detection bounds, including when the parent timeline window clips a mark. A shared endpoint has zero overlap. Preserve separate detections of the same behavior and show confidence as a detector score tied to its original detection. Use a compact span diagram only if the exact spans and overlaps are also readable as text. In **Supporting measurements**, group the recorded cue or metric, value and unit by detection. Use approved catalog labels. Retain the original evidence when the driver disagrees. |
| Warning | Exact issue time, recorded severity, and the named behaviors that contributed to this warning. Show the warning’s cue or qualifying duration when recorded and acknowledgment time when available. | Use the warning’s linked detection IDs. Keep the issue time distinct from detection onset and acknowledgment. Show the original related incident and its evidence when the link resolves, while keeping the warning selected in the panel header and timeline. Map severity through the recorded warning policy version. |
| Emergency Mode entry | Exact selected marker time and phase or outcome, recorded sequence context and latest channel results grouped by contact order. Show the latest recorded update time per attempt. | Present recorded milestones in an ordered list with explicit elapsed times for session events and dates, times and zone for provider updates that can occur after the session. Start and deadline carry their distinct labels. A configured deadline is a bound. In **Delivery updates**, show authorized timestamped history when supplied. Keep the originally selected marker steady if a later callback changes the outcome. Use approved historical contact labels or **Contact 1** and **Contact 2**. Show the authorized location only while eligible, with **Location unavailable** when appropriate. Display incident or clip evidence from an emergency entry only when the relationship is established by the record. |

For emergency outcomes, **Pending** means an in-progress attempt, **Accepted** means the provider took the alert for processing, **Delivered** requires confirmation, **Failed** means terminal failure and **Unconfirmed** means an attempted channel remained unresolved at the deadline. **Withheld** means the channel was prevented from sending. Show its recorded reason when available. **Canceled** describes the sequence and any effect on unsent attempts. Previously recorded attempts retain their own outcomes. A later provider update changes the evidence without reopening the sequence or moving focus. Use text, tabular figures where available, and a quiet static connector for the ordered milestones. [D04 outcome semantics](../../SCREENS.md#d04-emergency-mode) · [Emergency delivery rules](../../EXPERIENCE.md#emergency-mode-and-delivery-evidence)

**Clip evidence.** For an authorized linked clip, show its state and exact expiry in the displayed zone. Use **Open clip** for playable clips and label a truncated recording as the available first segment. Describe pending capture or transfer using the granularity the service supplies. For a known unavailable clip or a documented capture decision, use the recorded reason, such as expired, deleted, recording declined, behavior excluded or a storage or transfer limit. When the reason is absent, use **Clip unavailable** or **No linked clip** according to the record. Keep incident evidence visible throughout media loading, failure and removal. [Clip replay](../../SCREENS.md#d11-clip-replay) · [Data lifecycle](../../TRD.md#4-authentication-authorization-and-security)

## Selection, focus and recovery

1. **Open from a marker or row.** Keep keyboard focus on the activating control. Mark the same occurrence in both views and repeat its label and timestamp in the panel header. Give the selected row and timeline a reachable **Read selected details** jump that deliberately focuses the panel heading. Make marker and list entry accessible names include event type, recorded label and exact elapsed time. Preserve visible focus independently of the selected styling. Distinguish two events at the same time by source identity and label.
2. **Move within the session.** Anchor selection to the session and exact incident, warning or emergency occurrence identity. On another selection, update the heading, marker, row and loading state together. Ignore late results for an earlier selection. Keep the selected time in the header if a range change excludes it. **Back to selected time** returns to the corresponding marker or row and fetches the containing window if necessary. A timeline-window failure gives an inline retry with the selected time intact.
3. **Read and close.** Use a structured heading, ordinary links and buttons, and a semantic disclosure with a visible expanded state. Tab follows reading order. Disclosure activation uses Enter or Space. **Close details** restores focus to the activating marker or row when present, otherwise to its equivalent visible occurrence control or the timeline heading. Escape can offer the same action when focus is inside D08 and a nested control has no prior claim. Preserve saved range and location on close. Provide the ordered event list and exact values without hover, drag or reliance on color.
4. **Open D11 and return.** Preserve the D07 range, exact selected occurrence, related incident context and original trigger. On authorized Back, reopen the panel, restore the range and occurrence, and focus **Open clip** if still available, otherwise the clip section heading or status. **Back to selected time** remains available. If access to the session has since been lost, follow D07’s generic unavailable path and clear the private evidence. [ARIA disclosure pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/disclosure/) · [RAFIQ accessibility](../../EXPERIENCE.md#accessibility-floor) · [Reflow guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html)

| State | Panel behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Fresh selection or loading | Show the selected type and time immediately from the authorized marker. Announce loading once. Use a still placeholder only for evidence being retrieved. |
| Partial evidence | Keep verified facts visible. Distinguish no overlap, no recorded measurement, no linked warning, no linked clip and unknown availability according to the response. Preserve zeros as values. |
| Temporary failure or offline review | Keep the authorized D07 context and time. Offer a scoped retry for the affected evidence or media metadata and preserve the occurrence. |
| Delivery or media status changes | Update the affected row in place. Announce a meaningful change once, without rereading the panel or moving focus. If a focused clip action becomes ineligible, move focus to the updated clip status. |
| Record unavailable or access revoked | Clear any affected private data and use the appropriate generic unavailable feedback. If the D07 session remains authorized, retain only the authorized context. For a lost session, use the P08 outcome with **Back to Sessions**. |

At laptop width, 200% browser zoom and 320 CSS px reflow, allow wrapping for dates, labels, units and actions. Use stacked list items for measurements and delivery details. The panel fits the page’s width and shares its vertical scroll. Any horizontal scrolling needed for the parent plot stays in its named plot region. [Shared screen rules](../../SCREENS.md#shared-design-rules)

## Contract checks before implementation

- **Occurrence identity.** M03 `getIncident` returns detections and warnings by incident ID. A warning uses its warning ID, and emergency markers expose sequence ID, elapsed time and state. Confirm an immutable occurrence key for each marker in a sequence, including same-time markers, and the D07 range/selection return contract. Obtain explicit emergency-to-incident or clip associations before showing those links. [Session and timeline operations](../../API.md#7-detailed-endpoint--operation-specifications)
- **Warning cues and durations.** `Warning` links detections but lacks a warning-time cue or qualifying duration field. Present linked detection evidence as recorded detection evidence. Confirm whether the issued warning explanation is retained and retrievable. Use the versioned catalog for measurement labels and the recorded values and units. [API schemas](../../API.md#7-detailed-endpoint--operation-specifications)
- **Delivery history and location.** `getEmergencySequence` includes sequence start, deadline, current state and up to four attempts with channel, contact order, current status and `updatedAt`. Confirm an authorized history of sequence transitions and channel updates, including withheld reasons, event timestamps and ordering. Until then **Latest recorded outcomes** is accurate. The endpoint excludes coordinates and destinations. A separate authorized data contract is required to show any location, with current eligibility and the 24-hour expiry enforced. [Emergency operations](../../API.md#7-detailed-endpoint--operation-specifications) · [Retention and privacy](../../EXPERIENCE.md#retention-privacy-and-export)
- **Clip reasons and expiry.** `getIncident` exposes clip ID and coarse state. `getClip` provides expiry, truncation and availability metadata for an authorized clip ID. Confirm which historical capture reasons survive when a clip was never created or its reference is gone. Use the available authorized metadata then, and avoid inferring historical consent, deletion or expiry from a generic failure. [Clip operations](../../API.md#7-detailed-endpoint--operation-specifications)

## Acceptance and approval

- Activate same-time incident, warning and emergency entries by keyboard and pointer. In every case, the exact selected identity and timestamp agree across header, marker and ordered list. Test a rapid switch during loading, close from the panel, and Back from D11 after the clip has expired.
- Review overlapping detections, an endpoint touch with no overlap, multiple warnings, missing measurements, and an empty versus pending clip. Verify separate timings, labels, units and precision. Add a late callback to a closed sequence and verify selection, focus and sequence state remain steady. Exercise canceled before send, withheld, accepted, delivered, failed and unconfirmed outcomes, and expiry or revocation of authorized location.
- Use only the keyboard at normal width, at 200% zoom and at 320 CSS px. Verify visible focus, no obscured content, equivalent event selection, scoped status announcements, and the same evidence and actions with reduced motion.

**Approval to record.** The inline layout and progressive disclosure are confirmed. The remaining choice is standard RAFIQ components with the proposed selection accent, the fully static version, or specific external component IDs from the table.
