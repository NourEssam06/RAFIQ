# D03 Monitoring

## Approved direction

The driver enters from D02 after admission or returns to active monitoring through manual resume. The workspace answers three questions in order. Is observation usable? What behavior has RAFIQ observed? What is happening with recording and sync? The driver uses the controls while stationary. An authorized supervisor has the delegated controls permitted by the approved procedure during supervised motion.

- **Approved layout A.** Put a full-width, inline warning region immediately below session and observation status. Place the stationary preview and supporting statuses beneath it.
- **Approved motion D03-C02.** Use GSAP for one brief accent on a noncritical stationary status change. State text, icons, warnings, and controls update immediately. The supervised motion presentation is passive.

## Stationary frame

1. Use the compact D02 workspace header and the **Monitoring** page heading. Show **Stationary session** or **Supervised session** and lead with **Monitoring active** or **Monitoring paused**, followed by the current observation state. Session type and current presentation are distinct. A supervised session can be stationary.
2. Use one inline warning region across the full workspace. In the quiet state it carries one short sentence. During a warning it carries a severity label and non-color cue, the behavior heading, one row per qualifying behavior, and **Acknowledge warning** when applicable. This region also carries the reason when whole observation is lost. Keep it in the same place through transitions and let it grow for longer explanations.
3. Keep the stationary session actions immediately below the warning, before the preview. Show **Pause monitoring** while active. In the paused variants use **Resume monitoring** in the same position, with the prerequisite beside it until the view is usable. Keep **End session** visually distinct from Pause or Resume. Place **Stop recording** beside the Recording status when applicable. Show any available supervised authorization and driver revocation in stationary context.
4. Below that, put **Camera preview** on the left and a vertical stack of **Recording**, **Sync**, and eligible **Emergency Mode** on the right, starting around a 60/40 split. An actionable D04 countdown takes priority in the top warning area as a distinct, labeled block with direct cancellation. Keep one reachable **Cancel contact alert** action. An eligible resting or outcome status sits with the supporting statuses. An ineligible session keeps ordinary warnings available and explains emergency eligibility in D02.
5. Use the existing dark canvas, cream ink, stone secondary text, Mori or the approved Space Grotesk fallback, and shared pill and outline controls. Start the 1440 px frame with 48 px gutters, `{spacing.xl}` between main regions, `{spacing.md}` inside panels, and `{spacing.base}` or `{spacing.sm}` between related lines. Use `{rounded.md}` for panels. Treat `{colors.accent-orange}` as a proposed warning accent for the icon or rule alongside cream text, severity wording, and an icon shape. Give escalation stronger wording, a more prominent severity icon and stronger border emphasis. Enhanced visibility increases warning text and non-color cue prominence. Verify contrast and focus in the actual design.

The alternate stationary layout put warnings in a left column beside a larger camera preview, with statuses below. That arrangement gives framing more room and echoes D02 setup. The approved full-width warning makes space for overlapping behavior cues and keeps the warning hierarchy consistent when the layout stacks.

## Observation and warnings

| State | Visible result and interaction |
| --- | --- |
| Usable observation, quiet | **Monitoring active. No qualifying behavior observed.** Keep the steady preview, current recording and sync states, and eligible actions. This is an observation result about the current view. |
| Usable observation, initial warning | Immediately show **Warning**, a visible warning icon, the observed behavior, cue and duration, and **Acknowledge warning**. Optional warning audio follows the driver’s D20 setting and applicable supervised procedure. |
| Combined warning | Use one warning, for example **Drowsiness and phone use observed**. Show separate lines such as **Drowsiness · Sustained eye closure · 4 s** and **Phone use · Active interaction with a phone · 3 s**. These durations are illustrative. Allow one through all enabled behaviors and wrap complete labels at narrow widths. |
| Escalated, acknowledged, recovered | Emphasize **Escalated warning** immediately when the versioned policy escalates. Acknowledgment preserves the underlying observation and leaves D04’s countdown state unchanged. Display acknowledgment while relevant. Policy determines warning persistence, recurrence, tolerated gaps, sustained recovery and cooldown. Keep an incident through approved short gaps and close it on sustained observed recovery. |
| Partly usable observation | **Monitoring active. View partly usable.** Name each affected behavior and the missing cue when known. Example “Phone use is unavailable because your hands aren’t visible.” Keep usable behaviors active and keep their warnings readable even when a different class is unavailable. Distinguish unknown cause from a known corrective cue. For a quiet partial view, scope any no-qualifying-behavior copy to the observable behaviors. |
| Deliberately paused | **Monitoring paused · Paused manually.** Keep **End session** available to the stationary driver. Enable **Resume monitoring** when the view is usable. Exclude the deliberate pause from elapsed monitoring exposure. Show camera and recording status from their own state. |
| Observation-loss pause | Immediately replace the live warning with **Monitoring paused** and the known reason. Design reason variants for obstruction, poor lighting, unreliable view or low confidence, and camera loss. Stop warning escalation and clear emergency qualification and the countdown. Include this gap in elapsed monitoring exposure. If the view remains unavailable, give the correction beside the preview and explain the resume prerequisite. |
| View restored | **View restored. Monitoring paused.** Require the stationary driver to select **Resume monitoring**. Start fresh behavior and emergency qualification after explicit resume. Show the quiet active state only after there is a usable observation. |

Use the release-qualified subset of **Drowsiness**, **Distraction**, **Phone use**, **Eating**, and **Drinking**. Use observable cues including sustained eye closure, sustained off-task gaze, active interaction with a phone, and a hand-to-mouth action in the context of food or a drink container. Reaching remains a release candidate. The approved policy supplies severity and timing. D03 displays categorical observation quality and cue durations. Coverage charts and analysis belong to D07 and D09.

Reuse D02’s existing stationary preview and four-corner framing guide. The Pen reference is the **Camera preview** group (`xu2eV`), with **Static framing guide** (`GEslg`). Keep the supplied video proportion, center the preview without cropping, and position the guide relative to the displayed video area. Use contrasting edges against light and dark frames. The guide indicates framing, with an explicit text result for observation quality below the preview. During camera loss replace the image with a labeled unavailable state. When a live feed is still available during poor light or obstruction, show the correction beside the preview. Put warning text outside the image and show **Live preview stays on this device** beside the camera. ## Recording and sync

| State axis | Visible result and interaction |
| --- | --- |
| Recording off or enabled | After a D02 decline show **Recording off for this session**. With current effective consent, show **Clip recording on** while eligible incident recording is armed, and **Capturing incident clip** during actual capture. Use a steady icon and text. A nonmatching behavior category has no expected clip. Expose **Stop recording** while applicable and show **Recording stopped** when capture stops. |
| Clip queue, upload and outcome | Show **Clip queued**, **Uploading clip**, **Clip available** or **Clip unavailable** as applicable, independently of capture and session record sync. An interrupted transfer returns to a pending or retry state if still eligible. Explain permanent unavailability where known, including queue or storage limits, expiry, or consent withdrawal. Mark a bounded first segment as truncated when applicable. |
| Queue limit | At **100 MB or 20 clips**, whichever comes first, mark affected new clips unavailable and keep local monitoring and warnings running. Keep the status of previously queued eligible items visible. |
| Consent withdrawn | Stop capture and discard unsent clips, including pending transfers. Reflect confirmed loss of eligibility and prevent reconnect from restoring those items. |
| Sync current or backlogged | Use labeled text such as **Session records up to date** following durable acknowledgment. Distinguish **Records waiting to sync**, **Syncing session records**, **Sync delayed**, and **Some records unavailable** where applicable. If accurate item counts exist, they can accompany labels. Keep clip backlog independently labeled. |
| Offline or service unreachable | In the configured open tab, preserve local observation and warnings while the session remains active and the view is usable. Pair **Offline** or **Sync unavailable** with the actual queue state. Use copy such as “Monitoring continues on this device. Session records are waiting to sync.” If paused, keep the pause as the headline. The post-readiness offline qualification target is up to 30 continuous minutes. |
| Reconnecting and account check | On reconnect, verify the same eligible driver and retry eligible records and clips. Show **Checking account** or **Syncing** according to the actual operation. Confirm sync after the relevant receipts. A confirmed restriction follows P07 and keeps uploads locked. Sign-out clears private state and locks queues to the owner. |

Keep network reachability, durable session-record receipts, clip availability, and D04 provider delivery distinct. A browser network hint can inform offline copy. Service and queue results supply the sync outcome. Use steady text and icon shapes for each status, with separate marks for waiting, transfer, confirmed success and unavailability. ## Supervised motion, exit and recovery

- Use the institution-approved passive presentation. Show observation and warning information prominently in fixed reading order, with compact steady recording, sync and eligible D04 statuses subject to the procedure. Remove stationary preview and navigation, including their focus targets. Suppress animated feedback. Use stationary or simulator fixtures for design and review.
- Only the authorized supervisor on the driver’s device can use **Acknowledge warning**, **Cancel contact alert**, or **Pause monitoring** when applicable during motion. Keep start, resume, end, settings and delegation with the stationary driver. Revoked or absent authorization removes delegated controls. The exact passive display and authority for entering and leaving that presentation come from the approved procedure.
- Keep D04 an embedded region. Plan space for **Standing by**, an active countdown with a readable remaining time and direct cancellation, **Canceled**, **Withheld** or **Closed**, and channel-level **Pending**, **Accepted**, **Delivered**, **Failed**, or **Unconfirmed** as appropriate. The acknowledgment control remains separate. Cancellation, observed recovery, camera pause and ending stop unsent attempts. Fresh active-tab authority governs each attempt, and reconnect leaves stale sequences closed. D08 owns later channel history.
- While stationary, an in-app route that would leave offers **Stay in session** and **End session**. Start focus on **Stay in session**, contain focus while the confirmation is open, and return focus on dismissal. Keep urgent D04 cancellation directly reachable under the approved procedure. End stops the camera, capture and unsent emergency actions and releases delegation. Open D05 for end status or ready D07 when the required records and analytics are ready. Optional clips can remain pending. Closing, reloading or crashing ends live observation and follows D05’s same-driver recovery from the last durable observation.

## Pen.dev representation and acceptance

- Use editable region, text, icon, button, preview, and guide layers. Start with a 1440 px stationary frame, a 900 px laptop variant, a 320–390 CSS px zoom reflow variant, and a conditional passive supervised fixture. Use a staged image for a usable preview and a neutral labeled placeholder for camera loss. Show the GSAP accent as two visual states with a timing annotation. The frontend owns camera permission and stream, observation, gating, local audio, real sync and recording outcomes, focus handling and the contextual GSAP trigger.
- Keep reading and keyboard order aligned. On reflow, stack session and observation, warning and urgent D04 action, session controls, preview and guidance, then the supporting statuses. Make applicable controls reachable without horizontal page scroll at narrow laptop widths and 200% zoom. Check 320 CSS px reflow, long reason text and multi-behavior wrapping. Avoid fixed chrome over warnings, actions or focused elements.
- Use headings and landmarks, visible keyboard focus, text and shape alongside color, and clear labels for acknowledgment, cancellation, pause, resume and end. Announce meaningful warnings or escalation and essential pause or countdown changes once. Use polite announcements for routine status transitions. Keep warning durations and D04 time readable on demand. Avoid announcing predictions, durations, percentages or timer ticks repeatedly. Preserve focus during state updates and use a logical surviving control if its current target disappears.
- Cover each observation row and state axis above using frame and component variants. Verify **combined warning → escalation → acknowledgment**, **warning → feed loss → view restored → deliberate resume**, **partial view + warning for a usable behavior**, **deliberate pause → resume**, and **offline while active → reconnect → pending upload with required records synced**. Exercise queue cap or expiry, consent withdrawal while transfer is pending, and interruption into D05. Test supervised authorization and revocation with the driver passive, including independent acknowledgment and D04 cancellation. Check that emergency cancellation remains directly accessible and a stale countdown closes after camera loss or end.
- Compare warning readability with audio off, in Enhanced visibility, at browser zoom and with reduced motion. Verify zero accent motion during supervised presentation and that the stationary accent alters only the secondary highlight, never critical copy, controls or timing.
