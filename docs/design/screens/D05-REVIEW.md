# D05 End status and interrupted-session recovery

Proposal for approval · Operate · Driver desktop web workspace

## What the driver needs to know

The driver arrives after ending observation, opening a pending session from D01 or D06, or recovering an interrupted run. Lead with **Session ended** or **Session interrupted**, followed by **Camera off** once the local stop or the session's ended state is known. Identify the run by local date, start time and time zone. Show the confirmed end time when available. The heading describes what happened to observation. The three rows below describe what is happening to the saved data.

**Confirmed interaction decisions.** Keep D05 open when analytics becomes ready and reveal **Open session details** (D07) in place. After interrupted recovery completes, retain **Interrupted** and move the explanation into **How this session ended**. Keep **Back to Sessions** (D06) available in every authorized D05 state and restore the list context when returning from D06. D03 can still go straight to D07 if review is ready at entry. **Start session** in the private navigation opens D02 for a fresh readiness check.

## Status and recovery

Use a read-only checklist with a fixed order and a distinct label, icon and status in every row. The record row covers sync and finalization. Analytics waits for confirmed required data. Clip upload has its own state and can still be queued, uploading or unavailable when review opens. Use a count only when the application knows what it covers. D05 uses status text and, where available, timestamps or transfer counts. The incident timeline, metrics and clip playback live in the review surfaces.

| Row | Active or pending | Ready or empty | Interrupted, retryable or terminal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Session records** | **Waiting for connection**, **Checking account**, **Syncing session records**, or **Confirming session end** once required items are acknowledged and finalization is pending. Keep the reason next to the row. | **Session records synced** after required durable receipts and finalization are confirmed. | **Sync delayed** for eligible work to retry. **Some records unavailable** for confirmed partial loss, or **Session records unavailable** if affected required data cannot be recovered. Explain an authorized reason where known. Analytics availability follows the confirmed outcome for required inputs. |
| **Analytics** | **Waiting for session records** until required sync and finalization succeed, then **Processing analytics** for a confirmed pending calculation. | **Analytics ready** reveals **Open session details** immediately. A ready review can still contain individual unavailable metrics. | **Couldn’t check analytics status** for a transient retrieval error. Offer **Check again** where eligible. Use **Analytics unavailable** with a safe, confirmed reason for a terminal outcome, such as insufficient usable observations or missing required records, if supported. |
| **Clip uploads · Optional** | **Clips queued**, **Waiting for connection**, **Uploading clips**, or **Transfer interrupted** if an eligible clip remains recoverable. Mixed example, **1 available · 1 queued**. | **Clips available**, **Recording was off**, **No clips were created**, or **No clips match recording settings**, using the cause only when known. | Show mixed results where known, for example **1 available · 1 unavailable**. Explain expiry, withdrawn consent or a queue/storage limit at the affected scope. A clip discarded before it entered the queue remains unavailable. Offer **Retry clip upload** only for eligible pending clips when the action is supported. |

- **Normal end.** Render the known camera-off state immediately after D03 confirms teardown. Hosted sync and finalization may still be pending. With no known state on entry from history, use a bounded **Loading session status** placeholder. If the lookup fails, use **Couldn’t load session status** and an eligible retry. Retain known authorized information during refresh.
- **Interrupted return.** Closing, reloading or crashing ends observation. Before fresh online sign-in by the same eligible driver, use the sign-in or restricted account surface as appropriate and keep the queue, session identity, timestamps and counts private. After verification, say **Monitoring stopped when this session was interrupted. We’re recovering saved records.** Finalize at the last durably recorded observation after replay of eligible records. Once confirmed, tuck the explanation into the disclosure and show the recorded endpoint when supplied. Where that endpoint is unavailable, omit the time and use only a confirmed recovery outcome. Reconnect never starts monitoring or revives an unsent emergency action.
- **Connection loss and retry.** Explain why progress paused and what the driver can do. When reconnect attempts are automatic, use **Waiting for connection** and say what will retry once eligible. Use **Sign in** only when fresh verification is needed. An available manual action targets one operation, such as **Retry record sync**, **Check again** for a status read, or **Retry clip upload**. On activation, change that row to **Retrying…** or **Checking…** and suppress duplicate submissions. Respect any service retry window. Success uses the confirmed row status. Failure returns the action if eligible and keeps the reason in place. If a focused retry disappears, move focus to the affected row heading. Background updates leave focus where it is.
- **Permanent or access-related outcomes.** A verified owner may see a safe reason for terminal sync, analysis or clip loss within an accessible session. End retries for expired, deleted or permanently ineligible items. If the session itself is no longer accessible, clear the private view and use P08 for the unavailable link, P07 for a restricted account, or the sign-in flow as applicable. Removal of a single optional clip leaves independently authorized metadata and review available.

Use an ordered list only if multiple authorized, timestamped events are available. With only the last durable observation, a single line is enough. For a measurable transfer, a labeled [native progress bar](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Reference/Elements/progress) is a simpler alternative to C03 and C04. The current API draft offers statuses and durable receipts. A reliable denominator, byte telemetry or estimate would need its own verified source. Use state text for sync, analytics and default clip upload. Byte transfer completion and published clip availability are separate confirmations.

For a chronological status display, [Aceternity Timeline](https://ui.aceternity.com/components/timeline) provides [public source](https://ui.aceternity.com/registry/timeline.json) built on React, Tailwind and Motion. Its beam follows page scroll, so its movement would communicate reading position. The static recovery list communicates event order with less runtime work and keeps the checklist independent of chronology. Use the source as a visual reference for dot-and-label spacing only.

**Motion recommendation.** Choose C00 or add C01 as a single confirmation accent after a real state change. Keep intermediate progress, retry errors and disclosure feedback steady and local. Avoid mount animations, looping progress effects, row reordering and focus changes on background updates. Cancel the decorative cue if the underlying state changes or access is lost.

## Accessibility and proof cases

- Use a main landmark, a skip link and a single H1. Focus the heading on navigation. Give the disclosure keyboard-operable expanded/collapsed states and keep it visible at zoom. If a focused recovery explanation changes to the collapsed disclosure, preserve access and focus on its summary.
- Pair every state with text and a non-color cue. Use a polite status region for meaningful changes such as **Analytics ready** and a failed user-initiated retry. Deduplicate announcements from polling and omit ticks and changing byte counts from routine live announcements. Progress semantics carry measured values when available. The ready link works immediately with reduced motion. [WCAG status messages](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/status-messages.html) · [W3C progress announcements](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/aria/ARIA25)
- For the eventual Pen handoff, show normal end with pending analytics, ready analytics with a clip still pending, offline while ended, interrupted recovery after verification, interrupted recovered with the disclosure both collapsed and open, transient retry and outcome, optional clip expiry or consent withdrawal, required data terminal failure, temporary status lookup failure, and continuation to the generic unavailable link. Use desktop and stacked examples. Pen can depict component states and animation endpoints. Frontend verification covers actual authorization, queue replay, live announcements and reduced-motion behavior.

## Contract questions before implementation

1. The draft `Session` response has `lastDurableObservationMs` and a lifecycle state of `active`, `syncing` or `ended`. The finalization input distinguishes interruption. The read model omits that provenance. Confirm how authorized D05 and D06 recover **Interrupted** on a later visit and what the application should report if no durable observation exists.
2. Confirm which statuses are derived locally and which are returned by hosted reads, particularly mixed clip cohorts, no-clip reasons and terminal record loss. Check status reads, response deadlines and the scope of manual record/clip retry. **Check again** refreshes the analytics status. Show product actions and reason-specific copy only when confirmed evidence supports them.

## Approval choices

Choose **A** or **B** for layout and **D05-C00** for a static screen, **D05-C01** for the GSAP accent, or **D05-C02** for the Motion accent. C03 and C04 are conditional alternatives for future measured clip transfer. The proposed custom components are the **three status rows** and **interruption disclosure**, with a static event list only where confirmed events exist.
